# Entry Quality Rubric

## Purpose
Score the **quality of the entry**, not the recommendation and not the eventual P&L.

## Components
### Location Quality — 20
High scores require entry at a meaningful H1/M30 POI with clear structural or auction context.

### Liquidity Event — 20
Look for meaningful session/structural liquidity: Asian/London H/L, PDH/PDL, equal H/L, external liquidity.

### M5 Structure / Displacement — 20
Require evidence such as MSS/CHoCH/BOS and decisive displacement.

### Retest Quality — 15
Reward clean return to FVG/OB/displacement origin rather than chasing.

### VWAP / Volume Profile / Pivot Confluence — 10
Assess Daily Open, VWAP, VAH/VAL/POC, pivots and related auction context.

### Momentum / TDI Confirmation — 10
Use TDI/MACD/divergence/EMA as confirmation only.

### Entry Efficiency / Structural SL — 5
Reward clean invalidation with sufficient room to next liquidity objective.

## Grades
- A+: 90–100
- A: 80–89
- B: 70–79
- No-Trade Quality: <70

## Hard gates
A+ requires:
- valid location
- meaningful liquidity event
- M5 structural shift/displacement
- defined structural invalidation

If a required gate is missing, cap grade below A+ even if secondary indicators add points.

## Research rule
Historical scores are immutable. Evaluate later whether higher score bands correlate with improved expectancy, MFE/MAE and profit factor.
