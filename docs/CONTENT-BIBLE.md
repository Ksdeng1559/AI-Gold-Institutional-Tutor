## Starter campaigns
### 1. Correct direction, stopped first
Hook: “If your gold bias is right but your stop keeps getting hit first, your problem may be the entry.”
Value Bomb: Entry Quality Scorecard
CTA: DM SCORE
Diagnostic: “Which part fails most: location, liquidity, confirmation, retest, or risk?”

### 2. Entering before confirmation
Hook: “A 30-minute zone is not an entry signal.”
Value Bomb: 6-Step Confirmation Checklist
CTA: DM GOLD
Diagnostic: “Do you usually enter on the touch, after a sweep, or after M5 structure changes?”

### 3. Ignoring session context
Hook: “New York often makes more sense after you read what London already did.”
Value Bomb: NY Classification Worksheet
CTA: DM NY
Diagnostic: “Do you struggle most with Pullback, Continuation, Reversal, or Sideways days?”

### 4. FOMO / chase entry
Hook: “A profitable chase is still a poor entry.”
Value Bomb: Behaviour Audit
CTA: DM DISCIPLINE
Diagnostic: “Which behaviour costs you most: FOMO, early entry, chasing, or early exit?”

### 5. Outcome/process confusion
Hook: “A winning trade can still be badly executed.”
Value Bomb: A+ vs Bad Win Review Sheet
CTA: DM REVIEW
Diagnostic: “Do you score the setup before or after you know the result?”

### 6. Overusing indicators
Hook: “TDI can confirm an entry; it should not create the trade thesis.”
Value Bomb: Indicator Hierarchy Map
CTA: DM TDI
Diagnostic: “Are you using TDI for context, confirmation, or the entry trigger?”
